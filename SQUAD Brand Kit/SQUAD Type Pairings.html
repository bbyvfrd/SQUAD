BUILT
STRONG.

Five heavy sans-serif systems. Every candidate verified for Azerbaijani diacritics (ə, ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü), Cyrillic, and Latin Extended at the heaviest weight available. Italic axis dropped: pan-language italic at black weight is unreliable on Google Fonts. The spike is color now. One word in terracotta. Everything else in jet ink. Weight stays at full chunk.

Russo One

Body: Inter 400 / 500  ·  Mono: JetBrains Mono
Display heavyweight
Cyrillic-native block
CARVED FROM STONE
Specimen 04 · Russo One

SHOW
UP.

Russo One is a Cyrillic-first display font. Designed by Jovanny Lemonad with Russian as the home script. The Latin glyphs match the weight and structure of the Cyrillic exactly. Single weight, but feels carved out of granite. Use only in display, never in body.
