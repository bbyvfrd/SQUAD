Risk Note · The Reflex Trap

Inter is the AI default.
Inter Tight is not.

Inter is the most over-used sans of the last decade. Every SaaS landing page, every dashboard, every "modern" startup. PRODUCT.md explicitly rejects looking generic. So the question is fair: does choosing Inter Tight just rebrand the reflex?

The answer is: only if you use it like SaaS uses it. SaaS uses Inter at 400–600 weight, large line-height, generous letter-spacing, on white. SQUAD's commitment is the opposite of that: Inter Tight at 900 with negative letter-spacing, on warm linen or jet, with the terracotta color spike. Same family, different brand. The tightness, the weight, the spike, and the warmth of the color system together pull Inter Tight out of SaaS reflex territory.

If at any point a SQUAD surface starts to feel like a Linear or Notion screen, the cause will be not enough weight, not enough scale, or linen drifted toward white. Catch those, and the family stays in SQUAD's hands.
