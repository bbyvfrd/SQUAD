# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SQUAD is built for three connected groups in recreational sports:

- **Players** — solo players, newcomers, and people joining outside their usual circle. They want to find available games, trust the organizer, see who's confirmed, and join with clear expectations on cost, level, and venue. Today they live across Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, and friend networks.
- **Organizers** — the people who actually make games happen. They create games, fill slots, chase confirmations, handle dropouts, find replacements, and collect payment. Their reliability burden is the strongest problem signal in the category.
- **Venue operators** — pitch, court, and hall owners who want clearer demand, fewer no-shows, and lightweight booking without heavy admin.

The product is global by default — not tied to a city, country, or single sport.

## Product Purpose

SQUAD is an app that replaces scattered sports coordination with one place: discover games and venues, organize and join with trust, keep games from falling apart. It does not pretend the chat groups disappear — sharing into WhatsApp / Telegram / Facebook stays part of the workflow.

Success is simple: more games happen with less manual chasing. Players find games faster. Organizers spend less time herding people. Venues see clearer demand.

## Brand Personality

Five attributes that anchor every brand decision:

- **Simple** — direct, uncluttered, easy to grasp. No feature theater, no decoration that doesn't earn its place.
- **Reliable** — the brand carries the product's core promise: games will happen, the platform doesn't break.
- **Eye-catching** — visually arresting through type, scale, and color. The brand gets noticed without shouting.
- **Bold** — opinionated, decisive, no hedging. Strong stance, not safe.
- **Strong** — substantial presence. Solid, not delicate. Confident without being aggressive.

Aesthetic reference cluster: champions4good.club, pleybyney.com.br, thisisfc88.com, thegrind.nl — bold typographic sports brand work. Visual vocabulary, not product metaphor.

## Anti-references

SQUAD must not look like:

- **Football-app neon-on-black** — FotMob, OneFootball, betting apps. The category-reflex aesthetic for "football product." Reject.
- **Azerbaijan-government-portal cream-and-blue** — civic-portal stiffness, cream + flat blue. Reject.

## Design Principles

1. **Plain-speak, not corporate.** Direct copy, opinionated voice, no hedging or buzzwords. The brand says what the app does in clear language.
2. **Reliability is the headline.** Every surface implicitly carries the product promise: games happen, the platform doesn't flake. The category's strongest problem signal — the brand leads with it.
3. **Type carries the brand.** Visual distinction comes from scale, weight, and contrast in typography — not from gradients, decorative illustration, or chrome. Strong type doing the heavy lifting.
4. **Color is a tool, not a costume.** The accent is loud and intentional. Use it for emphasis and meaning, not as a decorative wash. Restraint makes the boldness land.
5. **Real people, real games.** Photography and copy show actual organizers, venues, players. Trust is earned by specificity, not stock polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Multilingual: English, Russian, Azerbaijani at launch.** Type system must support Latin Extended-A (AZ-specific glyphs: ə, ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü), full Cyrillic, and English. All three first-class. The product is global, so type and layout systems should accommodate more languages over time.
- **WCAG AA contrast** as the floor. The current kit's terracotta-on-linen pairing is the highest-risk combo — verify before locking for body or interactive text.
- **Reduced motion** respected — energy must have a calm fallback. Strong type and color still carry the brand without animation.
- **Outdoor / mobile glare** — users will check games on phones outdoors. Brand surfaces have to read in sun.
